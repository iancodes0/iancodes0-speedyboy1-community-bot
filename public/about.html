About Orbit

Search with a little
more intention.

Orbit was made by Karaas Botros to make web search feel calmer, clearer, and easier to return to.

The idea started with a simple observation: finding one useful answer should not feel like navigating a crowded airport. Karaas built Orbit as a focused search home for curious people who want less clutter between a question and a good result.
